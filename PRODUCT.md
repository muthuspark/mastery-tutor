# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners use Newton to study a topic, practice what they have learned, understand mistakes, and decide what to study next. The interface supports sustained reading and focused assessment on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Newton provides course creation, lessons, assessment, feedback, and progress tracking. Its product direction is evidence-driven adaptive learning: help learners demonstrate, retain, and apply knowledge, with recommendations grounded in recorded evidence. The requirements are defined in `loop-prd.md`; they are not claims of proven teaching effectiveness.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, literary, thoughtful. The existing Paper & Ink system uses a calm reading rhythm, warm paper, charcoal text, and restrained crimson actions. Preserve the established interface rather than introducing a new aesthetic. Visual details are documented in `DESIGN.md` and implemented in the existing components and tokens.

## Anti-references

The established design guide rejects generic AI marketing copy, emoji-based UI decoration, oversaturated colors, and repetitive feature-card grids. Learning screens should serve the learner's task rather than introduce marketing layouts or decorative distractions.

## Design Principles

- Ground progress and recommendations in actual learning evidence.
- Keep the next learning action clear and explain why it is recommended.
- Preserve courses, learner progress, profiles, and exports as the product evolves.
- Use consistent controls and headers, with a calm hierarchy suited to reading and practice.
- Treat uncertainty explicitly; avoid unsupported claims about mastery or teaching effectiveness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility and mobile usability are existing product requirements. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable hierarchy, and meaningful loading, error, and disabled states. No additional numerical accessibility conformance target is established here.

This context records the existing direction from `loop-prd.md`, `README.md`, and `DESIGN.md`; it does not introduce a redesign.
